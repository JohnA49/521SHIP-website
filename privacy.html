Privacy Policy

521-SHIP respects your privacy. This policy explains the basic information we may collect when you use our website.

Information We Collect

When you request a quote, book a pickup, contact us, or submit a form, you may provide information such as your name, phone number, email address, pickup location, destination, and shipment details.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to inquiries, prepare shipping quotes, schedule pickups, communicate about your shipment, and provide customer service.

Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services such as Calendly for pickup scheduling and Formspree for form submissions. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Information Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through our website. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at shipping@521ship.com.
